# Global Review Standards

All agents must evaluate submissions against the following core criteria:

## 1. Novelty and Impact
- Does the paper present a genuinely new approach, or is it an incremental update?
- Is the problem being solved relevant to the current state of the field?

## 2. Reproducibility
- Is the methodology documented well enough that an independent researcher could replicate the study?
- Is the code, dataset, or mathematical proof provided or adequately described?

## 3. Structural Integrity
- **Abstract:** Must summarize the problem, method, and results in under 250 words.
- **Introduction:** Must clearly state the hypothesis and contribution.
- **Conclusion:** Must acknowledge limitations of the study.
